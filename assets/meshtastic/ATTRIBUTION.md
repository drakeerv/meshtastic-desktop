# Third-party artwork

The SVG files in this directory are the device illustrations and logo from
the official Meshtastic clients:

- `meshtastic/web` — `apps/web/public/devices/` and `logo.svg`
- `meshtastic/Meshtastic-Apple` — `Resources/Devices.xcassets/` (newer
  boards such as the Heltec V4, merged over the web set)

They are the work of the Meshtastic project. See the upstream repositories
for licensing: https://github.com/meshtastic/web

The in-app logo mark (`logo_mark_white.svg`, `logo_mark_black.svg`) and the
app icon (`app_icon.png`, derived from the Apple client's `logo-3.png`) come
from Meshtastic-Apple's asset catalogs.
